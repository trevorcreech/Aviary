eff-wordlist.txt is included unchanged. The generator selects only lowercase
ASCII words of 3 through 8 letters. The resulting vocabulary contains 6,216
words. At the owner's request, this family device uses three independent
uniform choices (approximately 37.8 bits of entropy), separated by hyphens.
Passphrases are 11 to 26 characters long and fit the WPA2 length limit.
